GeoShock Monitor

Do geopolitical threats reach US inflation right now?

The answer depends on whether the cost-push channels (energy, food and import prices) are open. This page tracks the threat level, the channel state and the risk to inflation and growth, using the models in Fire Without Smoke re-estimated on the latest data.

Notes

  • Models: Kalamara (2026), Fire Without Smoke: Geopolitical Threats, Inflation Channels, and the 2023–25 Decoupling. Code and replication files: github.com/Elenikal/geoshock.
  • Data: Caldara–Iacoviello GPR; FRED; NY Fed GSCPI; Yahoo Finance; GDELT. Rebuilt daily by a GitHub Action; GIPI lags about three months because of IMF commodity-price publication.
  • Tail probabilities use a Gaussian matched to the median and the 10–90 range of the estimated quantiles. Estimates are in-sample, reduced-form associations, not forecasts with verified accuracy.
